Future Directions

Mind, Cognition and AI

Philosophy of mind, cognitive psychology and neuroscience each offer frameworks that can sharpen how we build AI. I want to work at that boundary, asking both what these fields tell us about intelligence and what AI in turn reveals about the mind.

Agentic AI and Language Models

Autonomous LLM-based systems that plan, use tools and act across multi-step tasks are becoming practical. I'm interested in understanding their failure modes as much as their capabilities, and in building systems that are reliable in deployment.

ML for Scientific Discovery

Machine learning can find structure that human analysis misses, especially in physical and biological data. I want to work on models that respect domain constraints while surfacing genuinely new patterns.
